# Features (user perspective)

## Automatic profile isolation
Create separate play setups without worrying that one pack will overwrite another’s worlds or mods.

## Health-aware Play confidence
Before launch, understand whether the selected setup looks healthy—or what category of issue needs attention.

## Integrated mod discovery
Find and add content without leaving the launcher’s product flow.

## Quiet when healthy, loud when blocked
Healthy states stay compact. Problems surface clearly with next-step oriented messaging.

## Desktop-native navigation
A stable side rail and consistent page headers keep orientation clear across Home, Profiles, Mods, Library, and Settings.

## Ready-to-play runtime mindset
The product treats Java/runtime readiness as part of the experience, not a forum scavenger hunt.

## Safe ongoing improvement
Users can receive product updates through an official channel designed around integrity and clarity.
